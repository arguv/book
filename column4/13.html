🚇 Cheapest (Public Transport – ~45 min, 40 Kč)
Walk (5–7 min) from Wenceslas Square 43 to Můstek metro station (Green Line A).
Take Metro A (green) → direction Nemocnice Motol.
Get off at Nádraží Veleslavín station (≈ 20 min).
From there, transfer to Bus 119 → direction Letiště Václava Havla (Airport).
Bus ride ≈ 15–17 min.
Arrive at Terminal 1 / Terminal 2 (bus stops directly at airport terminals).
👉 Use a 90-minute ticket (40 Kč) — covers both metro + bus. Buy in metro station (yellow machines) or via mobile app PID Lítačka.
🚌 Direct Express Bus (Airport Express – AE, ~40 min, 100 Kč)
Walk 15 min or take Metro C (red line) from Můstek → Hlavní nádraží (Main Railway Station).
From there, take the Airport Express (AE) bus directly to the airport.
Price: 100 Kč (separate ticket, not valid with regular PID tickets).
Runs every 30 min. Good if you have large luggage.
🚕 Taxi / Bolt / Uber (~25–35 min, 700–900 Kč)
Easiest but most expensive.
Order via Bolt/Uber app to avoid overpriced taxis.
