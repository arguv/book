Brake System Specifications

Front Brakes: 305 mm (12.0") ventilated discs (вентилируемые)

  • Ventilated discs have cooling channels to prevent overheating.
  • Typically use semi-metallic or ceramic brake pads for better performance.

Rear Brakes: 278 mm (10.9") solid discs (сплошные)

  • Solid discs are simpler and lighter but may heat up faster under heavy braking.
  • Often paired with semi-metallic or organic brake pads for balanced wear.

Common Brake Pad Materials:

  • Semi-Metallic – Durable, good heat resistance, but may produce more dust.
  • Ceramic – Quieter, less dust, better for daily driving but usually more expensive.
